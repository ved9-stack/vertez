Why Vertez

Most career advice starts with a job title. Vertez starts with you: what you can already do, what you care about and where you want to go. Then it works outward.

Good to know

Six ideas worth carrying.

01

Skills travel further than titles.

A job title describes one role. A skill can work in dozens of them.

02

Interests are data.

What you read, build and talk about without being asked points somewhere real.

03

Small experiments win.

A weekend project can teach you more than a month of deciding.

04

Direction beats certainty.

You don’t need the final answer. You need a good next step.

05

People are part of the path.

Mentors and peers open doors that applications alone rarely do.

06

Fit grows as you do.

The right path today can change, and your plan is allowed to change with it.

Myth or reality

Four things people get wrong.

MythReality
“My degree decides my career.”A degree opens doors, but skills, projects and curiosity decide where you go next.
“I need to be certain before I start.”Direction comes from trying things. Small experiments beat long deliberation.
“Changing fields means starting over.”Skills like analysis, writing and leading carry across almost every field.
“Passion should pick the job.”Interest matters, but so do your skills, your people and the daily work itself.

Questions

Good questions, short answers.

Who is Vertez for?

Students choosing a direction, and working professionals who want to grow or switch fields.

What do I need to start?

A few minutes. You share your stage and goal, then add the skills and interests you already have.

How are my paths chosen?

Vertez compares your skills, interests, goal and field with a broad career catalog, then ranks the closest paths and shows strengths, gaps and a practical first move. Fit scores are comparisons, not hiring odds.

Is my information private?

Your profile is saved in this browser. When Supabase is configured, a private anonymous account tied to this browser also stores it. Optional AI advice sends your stage, goal, field, skills, interests and follow-up questions to Claude; your name and email are not included. Account protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Read the privacy policy.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Edit them or start over at any time from the results screen.
